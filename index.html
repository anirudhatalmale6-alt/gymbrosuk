The Mission

Solving men's isolation
through the gym.

GymBrosUK isn't a fitness app with a community bolted on. It's the other way round. A quarter of men in this country are in touch with their mates less than once a month, and the gym is full of them — stood three feet apart, headphones in, saying nothing for an hour.

We put men who train the same way into crews of up to four. Same goal, same nights, same gym. You get a training crew, somewhere to share the wins and the bad weeks in The Forge, and something honest to measure yourself against in The Smith. The lifting is the excuse. The brotherhood is the point.

Straight answers

Before you ask.

I'm a beginner. Am I going to look like an idiot?
No. Every level is welcome and your experience is on your profile from the start, so the lads you're put with already know exactly where you are. Plenty of men on here are three weeks in.
Is it actually free?
Yes. Bro is the full brotherhood experience — Crew Finder, The Forge, The Smith, messaging — and it stays free permanently, for everyone. Bro+ adds gym discounts and a few extra tools on top.
Do I need a gym membership?
No. Independent gyms, chains, home setups — all fine. Set where you train and GymBrosUK works around it.
Is this a one-off session or an ongoing thing?
Both, but the point is the ongoing one. Crews are built to last — same lads, same nights, week after week. If you just want a spotter on Thursday, Quick Sessions covers that.
What if there's nobody near me yet?
You'll be told straight away rather than left staring at an empty screen. You can widen your search, join a wider-area crew, or grab your invite link and bring the lads you already train with.
Is my data safe, and who can see my profile?
You control how much of your profile is public. Report and block are built in and taken seriously, community guidelines are shown when you sign up, and your details are never sold on.
