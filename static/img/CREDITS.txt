Selection rules applied
-----------------------
- No recognisable third-party logos, wordmarks or gym branding in frame.
- No glossy fitness models. Ordinary men, unposed, in a normal gym.
- Graded to near-monochrome so photography reads as GymBrosUK's own rather
  than as stock.

Replacing these
---------------
Drop a replacement into this folder using the same filename and dimensions
(hero-crew.jpg 1100x1376, band-kit.jpg 1800x600). Nothing else needs changing.
